How an order flows

From cart to doorstep, handled for you

  1. 01

    Customer orders from one or many restaurants

    One cart can span several restaurants. Each restaurant gets its own sub-order and the customer sees one combined order with a status for each.

  2. 02

    Restaurant accepts and prepares

    Staff accept or reject, start preparing, and mark ready. The server enforces the allowed transitions so nobody can skip a step.

  3. 03

    Smart rider dispatch

    Offers go to the nearest 3 online riders, widening from 1 km to 2 km to 5 km. Each offer expires in 20 seconds. Acceptance is atomic, so two riders never get the same order.

  4. 04

    Live tracking

    The rider's route and position stream to the customer's order page in real time through Firebase.

  5. 05

    OTP-verified delivery

    The rider enters the customer's OTP to complete the order. Takeaway orders skip dispatch and are handed over at the counter.

FAQ

Before you buy

What do I get when I buy?+

The full source code for the backend, admin & seller web, customer web, rider app and staff app, plus setup and deployment documentation.

Which payment gateways work?+

Razorpay, Stripe and PayPal, as well as cash on delivery and wallet. You pick the active gateway in the admin panel.

Do I need technical skills?+

You need someone comfortable deploying a web app with Docker. Most integrations (email, SMS, storage, payments, maps) are entered in the admin panel rather than code.

Can I run it in my country and language?+

Yes. Set currency, timezone and enabled languages in settings. The customer site ships in English, Spanish and Arabic with right-to-left support.

Will it work for groceries or pharmacy?+

Not yet. The template is built for restaurant food delivery.
